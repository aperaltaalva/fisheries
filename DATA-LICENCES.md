## Fiscal documents and public registers

Annual receipts are transcribed from public fiscal documents issued by Kiribati’s **Ministry of Finance and Economic Development (MFED)**. The dashboard links the original documents and identifies pages and revenue definitions. Vessel-screen and historical-association research also draws on public **Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission (WCPFC)** and **Pacific Islands Forum Fisheries Agency (FFA)** registers.

These bodies and their source documents retain their respective rights. Public availability is not a blanket licence for redistribution. The dashboard publishes analytical aggregates and source references, not copies of the registers or unpublished research source files. Historical affiliations do not establish current ownership or official endorsement.

## Exchange rates

The saved revenue model uses **Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA)** exchange-rate publications, whose preserved source files carry **WM/Reuters** attribution. See [RBA exchange-rate publications](https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/frequency/exchange-rates.html) and the [RBA copyright and disclaimer notice](https://www.rba.gov.au/copyright/).

RBA’s general licence does not automatically cover third-party material. Source-specific and third-party rights apply, including to material derived from third-party inputs; no complete third-party clearance is claimed here. The dashboard retains the previously saved model outputs and does not republish the underlying exchange-rate tables.
